Company-Wide Overtime & Daily Cap Policy Standard

Overtime authorizations are set at the company level. A single approval covers every Specialist in the company.

Company-Level Overtime Rules & Non-OT Safeguards:
  • Company-Wide Scope: Overtime approvals are authorized at the company level. A single approval covers every Security Specialist in the company.
  • Strict Non-Overtime Priority: Specialists who do NOT incur overtime are ALWAYS assigned first before any specialist who requires overtime.
  • 6th Day Approval: Authorizes Specialists across the company to work on a 6th calendar day in the schedule week, provided they still get ≥8.0h rest turnaround.
  • 7th Day Approval: Authorizes Specialists across the company to work on a 7th calendar day, provided they still get ≥8.0h rest turnaround.
  • 12-Hour Cap: Authorizes Specialists to work up to 12.0 hours in a calendar day (e.g. multi-post rollovers or split shifts).
  • 16-Hour Cap: Authorizes Specialists to work up to 16.0 hours in a calendar day.
